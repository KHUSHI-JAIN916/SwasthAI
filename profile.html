What data is collected?

Demographics, chief complaints, symptom characteristics, medication history, adverse drug reactions, and AYUSH Prakriti notes entered during consultation.

Why is it collected?

Solely for clinical case documentation and practitioner decision support. The system never prescribes or diagnoses autonomously.

Who can access it?

Only authenticated AYUSH practitioners and authorized caregivers granted explicit consent by the patient.
